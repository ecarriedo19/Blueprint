# What is FP&A in Construction?

Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) is the backbone of financial management for any successful construction company. It goes beyond simple accounting by focusing on forward-looking strategies to maximize profitability and minimize risk.

For a construction company, FP&A involves three core activities:

1.  **Planning & Budgeting:** Creating detailed budgets for specific projects, accounting for materials, labor, subcontractors, and contingencies. This also includes company-wide financial planning for overhead and growth.
2.  **Forecasting:** Using current project data and historical trends to predict future financial outcomes. This helps answer critical questions like, "Will we have enough cash flow to cover payroll next month?" or "Is this project still on track to be profitable?"
3.  **Analysis:** Comparing actual results against the budget and forecast. This involves analyzing job costs, vendor pricing, labor efficiency, and other key performance indicators (KPIs) to understand what's driving profitability and where improvements can be made.

Effective FP&A allows construction companies to move from being reactive to proactive, making informed decisions that lead to sustainable growth.
